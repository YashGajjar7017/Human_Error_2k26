## Implementation Notes

### Animation Durations

- **Entrance animations**: 0.3s - 0.5s
- **Hover effects**: 0.2s - 0.3s
- **Modal transitions**: 0.3s - 0.4s
- **Stagger delays**: 0.1s per item

### Color Scheme

- Primary: #667eea
- Secondary: #764ba2
- Success: #28a745
- Warning: #ffc107
- Error: #dc3545
